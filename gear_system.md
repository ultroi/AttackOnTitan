## Equipment Categories

### 1. ODM Gear
- **Primary Function**: Movement and combat mobility
- **Components**:
  - Gas canisters
  - Wire launchers
  - Control handles
  - Body harness
- **Attributes**:
  - Gas efficiency
  - Wire length
  - Control precision
  - Durability

### 2. Blades

- **Types**:

  - Reinforced blades
  - Specialized blades
- **Attributes**:

  - Durability
  - Weight
  - Special effects

### 3. Gas Tanks
- **Primary Function**: ODM gear fuel
- **Types**:
  - Standard tanks
  - Extended capacity
  - High-efficiency
- **Attributes**:
  - Capacity
  - Efficiency
  - Weight
  - Refill rate

### 4. Uniforms
- **Primary Function**: Defense and protection
- **Types**:
  - Standard uniform
  - Reinforced uniform
  - Specialized uniform
- **Attributes**:
  - Defense
  - Weight
  - Durability
  - Special bonuses

### 5. Accessories
- **Primary Function**: Additional bonuses
- **Types**:
  - Badges
  - Patches
  - Special items
- **Attributes**:
  - Stat bonuses
  - Special effects
  - Set bonuses
