# Attack on Titan Game Bot - Missions System

## Overview
The missions system allows players to take on various challenges and quests in the game. Complete missions to earn rewards including Marks, Valor, permanent stat boosts, and special items.

## Mission Commands
- `/missions` - View available missions or check progress of active missions

## Types of Missions
The game features 15 different missions across various categories:

### Exploration Missions
- Scout's First March - Complete 150 explores outside the starting district
- Endurance Run - Complete 500 explores without returning to home location
- Reclaiming - Collect 100 Bricks to reconstruct the wall
- Stray ODM Gear - Collect 5 Stray ODM Gear parts during exploration
- Last Scout's Journal - Collect 10 Journals left by scouts during exploration

### Battle Missions
- Light Purse, Heavy Steps - Earn 10,000 Marks by defeating Titans
- Marksman in Training - Use Training Dummy 1 time
- Tactician's Notes - Use Battle Journal 3 times (in different fights)
- First Bounty Attempt - Use 1 Bounty Permit
- Valor's Proof - Defeat 2 bounty targets using Bounty Permits
- Master of Hunts - Complete 5 bounty missions with Bounty Permits within 3 days

### PvP Missions
- Sparring Rounds - Win 3 player battles in one day
- Temporal Gambit - Activate 3 Time Contract Scrolls in a row, survive 5 PvP fights under their effect

### Travel Missions
- Travel Test - Move between two adjacent checkpoints
- March of the Walls - Travel from an outer district to Stohess (cross at least 3 checkpoints)

## Mission Rewards
- Marks - In-game currency
- Valor - Premium currency
- Permanent stat boosts - Increase character stats permanently
- Special items - Training Dummies, Time Contract Scrolls, etc.
- Special abilities - Like emergency healing

## Mission Items
During exploration, you may find special items needed for certain missions:
- Wall Brick (🧱) - Used for the Reclaiming mission
- ODM Gear Part (⚙️) - Used for the Stray ODM Gear mission
- Scout's Journal (📔) - Used for the Last Scout's Journal mission

## Tips
- You can have multiple active missions at once
- Some missions have time limits
- Some missions have level requirements
- You'll receive notifications when you make progress on missions
- You can cancel a mission at any time through the missions menu
