# World and Exploration System

## World Structure

### The Three Walls
1. **Wall Maria**
   - Outermost wall
   - Highest risk level
   - Richest resources
   - Special titan encounters

2. **Wall Rose**
   - Middle wall
   - Medium risk level
   - Balanced resources
   - Training facilities

3. **Wall Sina**
   - Innermost wall
   - Lowest risk level
   - Basic resources
   - Safe zones

### Districts and Areas

#### Major Districts
1. **Shiganshina**
   - Starting area
   - Basic training grounds
   - Supply depot
   - Character creation hub

2. **Trost**
   - Intermediate area
   - Advanced training
   - Market district
   - Mission hub

#### Special Zones
1. **Echo Bastion**
   - High-level area
   - Rare titan spawns
   - Elite missions
   - Special rewards

2. **Hollow Exchange**
   - Underground area
   - Stealth missions
   - Hidden treasures
   - Special titan types

### Environmental Features
1. **Open Fields**
   - High visibility
   - Fast travel
   - Common titan spawns
   - Resource gathering

2. **Forests**
   - Limited visibility
   - ODM advantage
   - Stealth opportunities
   - Special resources

3. **Underground Cities**
   - Complex navigation
   - Rare resources
   - Special missions
   - Hidden areas

## Exploration Mechanics

### Travel System
1. **Energy/Stamina**
   - Consumed during travel
   - Affects combat readiness
   - Recovers over time
   - Can be restored with items

2. **Map Discovery**
   - Gradual uncovering
   - Hidden areas revealed
   - Special markers
   - Fast travel points

### Environmental Effects

#### Day-Night Cycle
1. **Day**
   - Normal titan behavior
   - Standard encounter rates
   - Full visibility
   - Regular resource spawns

2. **Night**
   - Aggressive titan behavior
   - Increased encounter rates
   - Limited visibility
   - Special resource spawns

#### Weather Systems
1. **Fog**
   - Reduced visibility
   - Increased ambush rates
   - Special titan behavior
   - Unique opportunities

2. **Rain**
   - Affects ODM control
   - Reduced titan detection
   - Special missions
   - Unique resources

3. **Wind**
   - Impacts ODM movement
   - Affects projectile accuracy
   - Special combat mechanics
   - Unique challenges

### Exploration Rewards
1. **Resources**
   - Common materials
   - Rare components
   - Special items
   - Currency drops

2. **Discoveries**
   - Hidden areas
   - Special titans
   - Unique missions
   - Secret achievements

3. **Progression**
   - Area unlocks
   - New missions
   - Special abilities
   - Rank requirements
