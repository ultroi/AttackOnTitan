# How to Use the PVP System

## Step 1: Starting a Challenge
1. Reply to another player's message with the command: `/pvp`
2. This will send a challenge request to that player
3. You must reply directly to a message sent by the player you want to challenge

## Step 2: Responding to a Challenge
If someone challenges you, you'll see a message with these options:
- **Accept 👍**: Begin the PVP battle
- **Decline 👎**: Reject the challenge
- **CombatDome**: Accept the challenge in a specialized arena
- **Cancel**: Only the challenger can use this to withdraw the challenge

## Step 3: Battle Begins
Once a challenge is accepted, the battle begins:

1. **Turn-Based Combat**:
   - Players take turns using abilities or attacks
   - The challenger always goes first

2. **Battle Interface**:
   - Shows both characters with HP and Gas bars
   - Displays current turn information
   - Lists available actions based on your character

3. **Available Actions**:
   - **Abilities**: Character-specific skills (cost gas, have cooldowns)
   - **Basic Attack**: Standard attack (costs 20 gas)
   - **Switch**: Change character (limited uses)
   - **Surrender**: Give up and end the battle

## Step 4: Using Abilities
1. Tap an ability button to use it
2. Some abilities will be grayed out if:
   - You don't have enough gas
   - The ability is on cooldown

## Step 5: Battle Progression
1. Each action consumes gas
2. Abilities and attacks deal damage or provide buffs/debuffs
3. Battle continues until one player's character reaches 0 HP or surrenders
4. If a player doesn't take an action within 3 minutes, they forfeit

## Step 6: Battle Results
When the battle ends:
1. Winner and loser are announced
2. Rewards are distributed:
   - XP, Marks, and Valor based on outcome
   - Both winner and loser receive some rewards
3. Stats are updated in player profiles

## Tips for PVP Battles
1. **Manage Your Gas**: Abilities cost gas, which doesn't regenerate during battle
2. **Use Cooldowns Wisely**: Powerful abilities have longer cooldowns
3. **Watch for Buffs/Debuffs**: Some abilities apply effects that last multiple turns
4. **Character Selection**: Choose your team carefully, as your first character is used in PVP
5. **Level Advantage**: Defeating higher-level players gives bonus rewards

## Viewing Your PVP Stats
Use the command `/pvp` (without replying to anyone) to see:
- Your total wins and losses
- Your battle rating
- Recent match history

## Common Issues
1. **"Already in a battle"**: You can't challenge or be challenged while in a titan battle
2. **"Player has not started the game yet"**: The person you're challenging needs to be registered with the bot
3. **"No characters in team"**: Both players must have at least one character in their team
4. **"Not your turn"**: Actions can only be taken when it's your turn
5. **"Must reply to a message"**: You need to reply directly to a message from the player you want to challenge
